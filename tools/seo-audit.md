# Technical SEO audit for your own site

**Use case:** before launching or after a redesign, crawl your site and get one row per page with status, redirect chain, title, meta description, H1, canonical, hreflang, Open Graph tags, images without alt text and broken links, plus a site summary. It obeys robots.txt and rate-limits itself, so point it only at sites you own or manage.
